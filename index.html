State unclaimed property is governed by Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Acts, where the State Controller acts as a perpetual custodian for owners. County funds, however, are governed by local and county government codes (such as California Government Code §§ 50050–50057). Under these statutes, uncashed warrants and excess funds that remain unclaimed for three years are published in a local newspaper of general circulation. If not claimed within 45 to 60 days following publication, they permanently forfeit directly into the County General Fund and cannot legally be recovered.
